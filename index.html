About Us

Eternal Transport launched in April 2024, a Turkish VTC on TruckersMP. We're all about building a friendly community where drivers can join us for convoys, share the joy of trucking, and build solid teamwork.

While we love showing off our VTC at events, our main focus is creating a great atmosphere for everyone. Our community enjoys other games too, like GTA V, CS:GO, and plenty of others, making our bond stronger outside of trucking.

What We Offer

Convoy Events

Regular Public & Private Convoys

We host and participate in weekly convoys across ETS2 and ATS on TruckersMP, bringing our fleet together with custom routes and organized dispatch.

Community

Multi-Gaming & Active Discord

Beyond virtual trucking, we play GTA V, CS:GO, and more together. Our welcoming Discord server is the vibrant heart of everything we do.

Fleet Showcase

Official Liveries & DLC Support

Ride in style with our official company paint jobs, customized fleet trucks, and regular photo shoots featured across social media.

Staff Team

Active and Supportive Staff

Our staff team works hard behind the scenes to keep everything running smoothly. They're always ready to help and make sure the community stays friendly and enjoyable for everyone.
