About Us

Who We are?

ETERNAL TRANSPORT™ has quickly established itself as a premier virtual trucking company on TruckersMP. Our commitment to professionalism, community, and excellence drives everything we do.

We're not just a VTC - we're a family. Our members enjoy multiple gaming experiences beyond ETS2, including Counter-Strike 2, GTA V, and various other titles. This diversity strengthens our bonds and creates a more vibrant community atmosphere.

History & Memories

Every milestone, celebration, and turning point captured like real-life memories hanging from the road.

April 29th 2024

FOUNDED & CREATED

Eternal Transport was founded and created by Kerim, Eren, and Yiğit.

1st July 2024

ETERNAL FIRST OFFICIAL CONVOY

Eternal Transport hosted its first official convoy, marking the start of our journey as a VTC.

August 22th 2024

ETERNAL IS OFFICIALLY VALIDATED

Eternal Transport was officially validated in TruckersMP, becoming a recognized VTC on the platform.

June 24th 2024 & Feb 2025

MILESTONE ACHIEVEMENTS

We reached 75 drivers, which was a huge milestone for Eternal Transport. Unfortunately, the count was later deleted from the TruckersMP VTC page by one of our former higher managers. After rebuilding the team, we proudly reached 50 drivers again in February 2025.

April 2025

OUR FIRST BIRTHDAY

Eternal Transport celebrated 1 year of success, teamwork, and unforgettable moments. It was a special moment for everyone who helped build the community.

May 2025

WE GOT VERIFIED

Just two days after our 1st Anniversary Convoy, Eternal Transport received great news and was officially VERIFIED by TruckersMP.

June 2025

WE REACHED 110 MEMBERS

Eternal Transport continued to grow and reached 110 members, showing how strong and active the community had become.

September 2025

NEW OWNER

After internal discussions within the Leadership and Management Team, Konsoloss (Kerim) resigned from the Owner position. The new Owner of Eternal Transport became Fr!qheR^ (Yiğit).

April 2026

OUR SECOND BIRTHDAY!

Two whole years already?! Time seriously flies when you’re on the road.

We just wrapped up our 2nd Birthday Convoy, and the turnout was absolutely insane—525 players and 85 VTCs joined the convoy! Who knows... maybe we do it all over again for Year 3?
